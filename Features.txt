IDEAPROOF AI — FEATURE SPECIFICATION & CAPABILITIES
Academic Research Idea Validation, Methodology Planning & Literature Grounding

1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Purpose: A professional academic SaaS tool designed for researchers, graduate 
  students, and principal investigators to stress-test research hypotheses, 
  blueprint experimental methodologies, and ground proposed ideas against real 
  peer-reviewed literature.
- Design Aesthetics: Inspired by the restraint, usability, and typography hierarchy 
  of Linear and Notion. Warm off-white canvas (#FAFAF8), dark charcoal typography 
  (#0F172A), thin neutral borders (#E5E5E0), subtle shadows, and crisp Lucide icons.
- Academic Rigor: Emphasizes falsifiability, reproducibility, and transparent 
  distinction between speculative AI hypotheses and verified scientific evidence.


2. AI INFERENCE ENGINE & MODULAR ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Active AI Provider: OpenRouter (OpenAI-compatible /chat/completions endpoint at 
  https://openrouter.ai/api/v1).
- Swappable Architecture: Modular abstraction layer (src/lib/ai-provider.ts) 
  allows switching between OpenRouter and Nebius Token Factory 
  (https://api.tokenfactory.nebius.com/v1) via environment variables without 
  modifying application or route code.
- Supported & Tested Models:
  * nvidia/nemotron-3-super-120b-a12b (Default: High-reasoning Mixture-of-Experts)
  * nvidia/nemotron-3-ultra-550b-a55b (Frontier MoE for complex multi-hypothesis reasoning)
  * meta-llama/llama-3.3-70b-instruct (Fast academic instruction-following)
- Environment Variables:
  * OPENROUTER_API_KEY: Server-side secret key (never leaked to browser bundles)
  * OPENROUTER_MODEL: Configurable target model identifier
  * AI_BASE_URL / OPENROUTER_BASE_URL: Optional custom endpoint override
  * NEBIUS_API_KEY / NEBIUS_MODEL: Alternative provider configuration
- Production Error & Exception Handling:
  * Rate Limits (HTTP 429): Clear warning with backoff advice
  * Authentication (HTTP 401/403): Guided prompts to configure keys in .env or Settings
  * Provider Outages / Timeouts (HTTP 502/503/504): Graceful user-facing diagnostics
  * Input Sanitization: Rejects invalid, empty, or malicious payloads (10-2500 chars)


3. STRUCTURED RESEARCH VALIDATION BLUEPRINT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every generated analysis delivers a publication-grade research brief containing:
- Academic Scope Calibration: Tailors depth and expectations across three tiers:
  * Undergraduate: Feasible within 1-2 semesters, bounded scope, clear baselines
  * Master's: Comparative methodology, formal hypothesis validation, standard benchmarks
  * PhD: Novel theoretical contributions, deep ontology gaps, stress testing
- Publication-Grade Title: Synthesizes a formal academic title suitable for thesis 
  or journal submission.
- Executive Summary: Multi-paragraph synthesis of core thesis, scientific value, 
  and timeline viability.
- Scientific Problem Statement: Formal critique of why existing computational or 
  experimental paradigms fail and the cost of inaction.
- Primary Research Questions (RQs): Specific, answerable, and falsifiable questions.
- Directional Hypotheses Pairs:
  * Null Hypothesis (H0)
  * Alternative Hypothesis (H1)
  * Theoretical / Physical Rationale
- Novelty & Gap Assessment:
  * Novelty Level Classification (Incremental, Moderate, Substantial, High)
  * Explicit Preliminary Notice disclaimer (mandating peer review before grant submission)
  * Concrete differentiation from prior published literature
- Chronological 3-Phase Execution Methodology:
  * Phase durations (e.g., Weeks 1-3, Weeks 4-7, Weeks 8-12)
  * Specific research activities per phase
  * Concrete deliverables per phase
- Established Baseline Comparisons: Standard benchmark algorithms, descriptors, or models.
- Dataset & Resource Specifications:
  * Required data types and modalities
  * Recommended open-access repositories and benchmarks (e.g., MoleculeNet, HuggingFace, NCBI)
  * Sample size and statistical power considerations
- Quantitative Evaluation Metrics & Statistical Validation:
  * Primary error and performance metrics (e.g., RMSE, MAE, R², F1, AUROC)
  * Statistical significance testing requirements (e.g., paired t-tests, permutation tests)
- Limitations, Threats to Validity & Risk Mitigation:
  * Methodological confounding variables
  * Data availability risks
  * Computational complexity constraints


4. VERIFIED ACADEMIC LITERATURE GROUNDING (OPENALEX INTEGRATION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Live OpenAlex REST API: Directly queries 250M+ cross-disciplinary publications.
- Zero-Hallucination Guarantee: AI hypothesis generation is strictly separated 
  from verified empirical literature. The system NEVER invents paper citations, 
  fake DOIs, or fabricated authors.
- Authentic Metadata Extraction:
  * Real, clickable DOI links (https://doi.org/...)
  * Genuine author rosters and publication years
  * Authentic journal/conference publication venues
  * Real citation counts in the global index
  * Open-access status badge and direct paper URLs
  * Verified abstract snippets


5. INTERACTIVE LITERATURE EXPLORER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Discovery Search: Query the global literature database by topic, keywords, or authors.
- Suggested Search Presets: 1-click curated searches across cutting-edge fields:
  * Contrastive learning clinical NER
  * Physics-informed neural networks Navier-Stokes
  * Knowledge distillation large language models
  * Retrieval-augmented generation hallucination
  * Spatio-temporal graph forecasting
- Advanced Literature Filtering:
  * Publication date range filter (From Year / To Year)
  * Open Access filter (toggle between all papers and freely accessible papers)
  * Result sorting (Relevance, Most Cited, Publication Date Descending)
- Instant Citation Generator: Copy formatted APA 7th Edition citations with 1 click.
- Seed Idea Analysis Workflow: Click "Seed Idea Analysis" on any discovered paper 
  to immediately pre-populate a new research validation request centered on extending 
  that publication.


6. EXPORT, PRINTING & SHARING CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  with GitHub Flavored Markdown, ready for Notion, Obsidian, or thesis repositories.
- Print-to-PDF Optimization: Specialized @media print stylesheets strip away sidebars, 
  headers, and buttons, formatting typography, margins, and section breaks for 
  flawless browser PDF generation.
- One-Click Link Sharing: Copy direct project URL to clipboard for peer review.
